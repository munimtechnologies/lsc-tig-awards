# LSC technology grants, 2016 and 2017: the lists, checked

The Legal Services Corporation (LSC) printed one description page per year for its
Technology Initiative Grant (TIG) rounds. For 2016 and 2017 those pages are gone from
lsc.gov: their old addresses now open the TIG index page, which has no list for either
year. This page reads them from the Internet Archive's June 2019 captures, sums every
list, and checks it against the press releases that are still on lsc.gov and against
LSC's own counts and dollar figures. It extends the 2018-2020 checks in
[tig-history.md](tig-history.md) back two rounds.

Data: `data/tig_2016_2017.json` (CC BY 4.0). Code: `tig_2016_2017.py` (Apache-2.0).
Read from the raw text of each document and hand-checked; the hand sums are pinned in
`test_tig_2016_2017.py`.

    python tig_2016_2017.py              # list sums, with gaps filled from LSC's releases
    python tig_2016_2017.py --sources    # each release's amounts against the page
    python tig_2016_2017.py --headlines  # LSC's statements against the lists
    python tig_2016_2017.py --summary    # headline figures as JSON

## The lists

| Year | Grants | Amounts on the page | Sum of the page's amounts | Gaps filled from | Sum with gaps filled | Grants with an amount on lsc.gov today |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 34 | 33 | $4,143,487 | TIG 16007 (PBIF per-grantee release) | $4,203,221 | 18 |
| 2017 | 25 | 25 | $3,927,974 | none | $3,927,974 | 25 |

- **2016: the page prints no amount for Iowa Legal Aid (TIG 16007).** The amount is in
  a different LSC document, the 2016 Pro Bono Innovation Fund release, whose Iowa section
  is headed "Iowa Legal Aid Receives $364,709 Pro Bono Innovation Fund Grant and $59,734
  Technology Initiative Grant". Without Iowa, the page's 33 amounts come to $4,143,487,
  which is $56,513 short of $4.2 million. With Iowa's $59,734 the 34 grants come to
  $4,203,221. That is $3,221 over the "more than $4.2 million" LSC states, and only the
  filled list fits it.
- **2016: the full list is not on lsc.gov any more.** The releases still there print
  amounts for 18 of the 34 grants. The other 16 are only in the archived page. (LSC's
  index page also links an Airtable view, "TIG Awards 2000-2020", which loads by script
  and could not be read as text.)
- **2017: the list adds up cleanly.** The archived page and the release print the same 25
  amounts, $3,927,974 in all, which fits "nearly $4 million". Unlike 2016, the release
  still lists every grant.

- **2016: Idaho's total is $10,000 below its own parts.** The per-grantee release is
  headed "Idaho Legal Aid Services Receives $322,919 in Technology Grants" and says "a
  total of $322,919", then names "its $251,985 grant" and "a second, $80,934 grant",
  which add to $332,919. The page prints the same two amounts.
- **2017: one heading carries the previous grantee's amount.** In the 2017 Pro Bono
  Innovation Fund release, Northwest Justice Project's section is headed "... $97,750
  Technology Initiative Grant". Its text says "a $185,250 Technology Initiative Grant",
  as the page and the TIG release do. $97,750 is the TIG amount in the heading just
  above it, for Legal Services of Northern Virginia.

| 2017 | release | nearly $4,000,000 | none | yes | yes |
| 2017 | PBIF per-grantee release | 25 grants | none | - | - |

All 9 statements fit their lists in every count, with organizations counted as distinct
printed names (27 in 2016, 22 in 2017) and states counted apart from American Samoa.
LSC's 2018 statements fit too ([tig-history.md](tig-history.md)), and every count
recorded there that misses its list is from 2019 or later. The dollar figures fit 2016
only once Iowa's amount is added. "About $4.2 million" is read as rounding to $4.2
million at the nearest $0.1 million, and "nearly $4 million" only as "less than $4
million".

## Limits

- The 2016 and 2017 pages are read from single Internet Archive captures, both from 11
  June 2019. That is two years and seven months after the 2016 announcement and 19 months
  after the 2017 one. Nothing here shows whether LSC changed the pages before then.
- Iowa's $59,734 is from one LSC release. No other document checked here prints it.
- Grants are as announced. Nothing here shows what LSC actually paid.
- States are read from each grantee's name and project description.
