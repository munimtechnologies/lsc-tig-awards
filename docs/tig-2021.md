# LSC technology grants, 2021: the page, the release and the total, checked

The Legal Services Corporation (LSC) announced its 2021 Technology Initiative Grant
(TIG) round on 17 November 2021: "35 Technology Initiative Grants (TIG) to 29 legal
services organizations totaling $4,268,938". Two LSC documents list the grants. The
[press release](https://www.lsc.gov/press-release/legal-services-corporation-awards-more-4-million-technology-grants-strengthen-legal-aid-low-income)
prints each amount in whole dollars, grouped by state; it is the source of the 2021
rows in `data/awards.json`, which sum to $4,268,931, $7 under the stated total. The
[2021 project description page](https://www.lsc.gov/grants/technology-initiative-grant-program/technology-initiative-grant-awards-tig-projects-funded-year/2021-tig-award-project-descriptions)
prints each amount to the cent, with an application number, in two sections: 19
Technology Initiative Grant awards and 16 Technology Improvement Project awards. This
page matches the two lists grant by grant, sums both against the stated total, and
checks LSC's cumulative "since the program's inception" figures in its 2020-2025 TIG
releases. It completes the run of checked rounds from 2014 to 2025 begun in
[tig-2014-2015.md](tig-2014-2015.md), [tig-2016-2017.md](tig-2016-2017.md) and
[tig-history.md](tig-history.md).

Data: `data/tig_2021.json` (CC BY 4.0). Code: `tig_2021.py` (Apache-2.0). Read from
the raw text of each document and hand-checked; the hand sums are pinned in
`test_tig_2021.py`, which also checks that the release's amounts are exactly the 2021
rows of `data/awards.json`.

    python tig_2021.py              # page and release sums against the stated total
    python tig_2021.py --sources    # amounts that differ and which figures make the total
    python tig_2021.py --headlines  # LSC's statements and cumulative figures
    python tig_2021.py --summary    # headline figures as JSON

## The two lists against the stated total

| Sum | Grants | Sum of the amounts | LSC's stated total | Difference | Rounds to the stated total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| description page | 35 | $4,265,737.84 | $4,268,938 | -$3,200.16 | no |
| release | 35 | $4,268,931 | $4,268,938 | -$7 | no |
| release's amounts with the page's cents | 35 | $4,268,937.84 | $4,268,938 | -$0.16 | yes |

| Page section | Grants | Sum | Smallest | Largest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technology Initiative Grant awards | 19 | $3,757,803.20 | $75,665 | $343,306.36 |
| Technology Improvement Project awards | 16 | $507,934.64 | $20,000 | $35,000 |

- **The page's list is $3,200.16 short of LSC's total.** Its 35 amounts sum to
  $4,265,737.84.
- **The release's $7 gap is cents.** The page prints cents for 9 grants, 6.84 dollars
  in all. The release drops them: for all 8 of those grants whose dollars it shares
  with the page, it prints the amount cut to whole dollars, not rounded (rounding would
  change 7 of the 8). Adding the page's cents back to the release's amounts gives
  $4,268,937.84, which rounds to the stated $4,268,938.
- **The total was made from the release's figures for the two grants where the
  documents disagree** (below). LSC's stated total fits them, not the page's.

| Page list with the release's amount for | Sum | Against $4,268,938 | Rounds to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| neither (the page as printed) | $4,265,737.84 | -$3,200.16 | no |
| Legal Services of Greater Miami | $4,268,737.84 | -$200.16 | no |
| Land of Lincoln Legal Aid | $4,265,937.84 | -$3,000.16 | no |
| Legal Services of Greater Miami and Land of Lincoln Legal Aid | $4,268,937.84 | -$0.16 | yes |

- Each pair differs in one digit: 174,774 against 177,774, and 26,678 against 26,878.
- Only with both of the release's amounts does the list reach LSC's total. This is the
  same pattern as 2018, where three LSC documents disagree on two grants and only one
  combination matches the stated total ([tig-history.md](tig-history.md)).
- Which figure LSC paid cannot be told from these documents. What can be said is that
  the stated total agrees with the release's two amounts, and the page's two amounts
  are $3,000 and $200 lower.

Other things on the page, none of which changes a sum:

- The page heads application AP-668 "Northwest Legal Aid", but its description begins
  "Northeast Legal Aid will develop a new app" for tenants in Massachusetts Housing
  Court, and the release lists the grant under Massachusetts as Northeast Legal Aid.
- Connecticut's amount is printed "$150, 593.60", with a stray space after the comma
  (seven 2014 amounts have the same stray space: [tig-2014-2015.md](tig-2014-2015.md)).
- Two grants have no application number (Legal Aid Society of Middle Tennessee and the
  Cumberlands; Neighborhood Legal Services Association, Pennsylvania), and one is
  numbered "AP19" where the other 32 read "AP-" and three digits.

## LSC's cumulative figures, 2020-2025

Each year's TIG release states how many grants LSC has made since the program began in
2000, and for how much. From 2021 on, each figure can be set against the one before
plus the round announced in between (grants and priced amounts as listed in
`data/awards.json`).

| Release | Previous release's grants | Grants in the round | Sum | LSC says | Difference | Previous 'more than' plus the round's priced grants | LSC says more than | Below the sum by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 826 | 33 | 859 | 859 | +0 | $81,679,135 | $81,000,000 | $679,135 |
| 2023 | 859 | 33 | 892 | 892 | +0 | $86,157,033 | $86,000,000 | $157,033 |
| 2024 | 892 | 32 | 924 | 924 | +0 | $92,306,081 | $91,000,000 | $1,306,081 |
| 2025 | 924 | 32 | 956 | 955 | -1 | $95,064,308 | $95,000,000 | $64,308 |

- **The grant counts chain exactly from 2021 to 2024**, so each release's figure
  includes the round it announces (unlike 2014's, which left out the round announced
  that day: [tig-2014-2015.md](tig-2014-2015.md)).
- **2025 is one short.** 924 plus the 32 grants the 2025 release announces (and that
  LSC's 2025 list shows) is 956; the release says 955. Nothing in LSC's documents
  explains the missing grant.
- **Every dollar figure in the table holds**, since each is a "more than". 2024's is the loosest:
  2023's "more than $86 million" plus the 2024 list's $6,306,081 already makes more
  than $92.3 million, against the stated "more than $91 million".
- **2020 to 2021 does not fit if 2020 counted like 2021.** The 2020 release says
  "nearly $70 million" and announces a $3,941,298 round. 2021's "more than $77
  million" less the 2021 round's $4,268,938 leaves more than $72,731,062 before 2021.
  If the 2020 figure included its own round, as 2021-2024's do, the two releases are at
  least $2,731,062 apart; if it left its round out, as 2014's did, they fit. Neither
  release says which.

## Limits

- Amounts are as LSC printed them. Nothing here shows what LSC actually paid, or which
  of two disagreeing figures is right.
- States are read from the release's state headings; organizations are counted by the
  release's names.
- The cumulative check treats "grants" as the same unit in every release, and reads
  "nearly" only as "less than". The 2025 round's sum leaves out one grant with no
  published amount (Legal Services of Greater Miami), so its dollar row is a lower
  bound.
