# LSC technology grants for national services

[tig-national.md](tig-national.md) set aside the seven Technology Initiative Grants (TIG)
that paid for LawHelp Interactive and noted that other grants may serve more than one
state too. This page lists those other grants: the ones LSC's own text says pay for a
named service that LSC calls national.

`data/national_services.json` (CC BY 4.0) lists each grant with LSC's words and a link to
the LSC page (or its archived copy), plus eight close calls left out and why. Each grant
matches exactly one grant in the round lists by year, state and amount.
`test_national_services.py` pins the hand sums; `test_national_services_doc.py` checks
this page.

## Findings

- **About a quarter of TIG dollars in 2014-2019 paid for national services** (25.2%, up to 27.5% with the part grants in full), against 6.3% in 2020-2025.
- **No grant in the 2023, 2024 or 2025 rounds names LawHelp Interactive or any of the five services.** The last was 2022's $598,970 to Michigan Advocacy Program to run LSNTAP "for the next two years".
- **LawHelp Interactive was most of it**: $4,648,850 of $6,764,724 in whole grants, 2014-2025.
- **Michigan's TIG total includes $1,068,295 for national services** (A2J Author 2017 and 2020, LSNTAP 2022); Maine's includes $225,250 for Stateside Legal, plus part of a 2014 grant.

## Limits

- The list rests on LSC's wording. A grant that pays for a national service LSC does not
  name, or does not call national, is not here. The search covered the saved text of
  LSC's releases and description pages for 2014-2025 (the services' names and the words
  "national", "nationwide" and "across the country"); it is not a reading of grant budgets.
- The closest call left out is 2019's $177,500 to Michigan Advocacy Program for "the legal
  aid community's guided interviews" and the National Subject Matter Index.
- "Part" grants have no national share unless LSC printed one. 2018's $297,175 is the
  description page's figure; LSC's 2018 release prints $267,175.
- A grant counts to its grantee's state in LSC's accounting. Calling it national is a way
  of reading the figures, not LSC's accounting.
